About El Nido

El Nido sits at the northern tip of Palawan, framed by dramatic limestone karsts rising straight out of turquoise water. It's the gateway to the Bacuit archipelago's hidden lagoons, secret beaches, and some of the best island-hopping in the country.

Things to do

01
Island Hopping Tour A

Big Lagoon, Small Lagoon, Secret Beach, Shimizu Island.

02
Nacpan Beach

A four-kilometer stretch of golden sand, quieter than the town proper.

03
Taraw Cliff Trek

A steep climb rewarded with a panoramic view of the bay.

04
Snorkeling at Snake Island

A sandbar connecting to a small island, ringed with coral.

Travel tips

Bring reef-safe sunscreen — regular sunscreen is discouraged near the marine sanctuaries. Environmental fees (₱200) are collected on arrival and cover several island-hopping stops. Cash is still preferred outside the town center.

Trip planning info

  • Getting there 1hr flight from Manila to El Nido, or 5–6hr van from Puerto Princesa.
  • Best time November – May (dry season)
  • Est. budget ₱3,500 – ₱6,000/day
